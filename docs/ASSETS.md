## Hero prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: hero illustration for SCOAI Lab, a supply chain optimization and AI research laboratory website. Create a refined, architectural isometric miniature of an interconnected supply chain: several ivory warehouse buildings, shipping containers, a small cargo truck, stylized trees, and a central abstract network of connected navy spheres, with fine navy routes connecting the buildings. Sophisticated editorial 3D illustration, matte ceramic and paper materials, finely crafted details, soft ambient occlusion, warm ivory background #f5f2eb, deep ink navy #07334b and muted blue #5b8c9f with tiny warm orange accents. Wide 3:2 composition, complete scene centered with generous breathing room, no bounding frame. Calm scholarly optimistic mood, elegant rather than toy-like. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. This is conceptual artwork, not a chart or factual depiction of a real facility.
